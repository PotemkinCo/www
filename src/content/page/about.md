I'm Alex (Alexander Potemkin). I write and build things under the name **Alex @ Potemkin&Co** — mostly software and systems work, with whatever else happens to be on the desk.

This site is a home for short notes and longer write-ups, plus a place to point at projects I ship or keep tinkering with. Expect posts about code, tooling, and the occasional detour.

### Bio

- **2001** — Developed custom CRM, connected company's offices via VPN, performed security audits.

- **2005** — Graduated. Joined Nokia (Siemens) Networks team to work on 2G & 3G networks.
- **2008** — Investment banking technical support at Deutsche Bank.
- **2011** — Made fintech work at Renaissance Capital.
- **2013** — Founded and managed cloud servers monitoring startup.
- **2014** — Transformed IT process of investment bank to enable merge into retail bank structure (ISO 20'000, ITIL, ITSM, etc.).
- **2017** — Created three social networks apps, Uber-Cargo-like service, assisted with digitalizing off-line media and corporate mobile learning and a few practical apps.
- **2018** — Mentored at TechStars, YellowDoor, Social Economy Fund, Skolkovo startup accelerators.
- **2020** — Built no-code platform for a Japanese market.
- **2022** — Founded NoCloud project.
- **2023** — Founded Devneya, an AI models mixture project.
